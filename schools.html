How to verify your school

  1. Enter the full street address in the district's school locator — boundaries split some subdivisions.
  2. Check for boundary changes. Fast-growing districts redraw lines when new schools open.
  3. Ask the listing agent for written confirmation, but trust the district over marketing copy.
  4. Call the school office if a home sits near a boundary street.

How to compare schools

Use the Kansas State Department of Education's KSDE Report Card for official assessment, graduation and attendance data, and visit in person. Rating sites summarise data differently — read their methodology before treating a score as definitive.

Tip for buyers: School assignment is one of the biggest drivers of 66221 home prices. A house a street away can cost noticeably more or less purely because of its boundary.
