Privacy policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains how 66221.com ("66221 Living", "we") handles information when you visit the site or submit a form.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — name, email, phone, address and project details you enter in quote, home-value, contest, pledge, application, listing, event, poll, newsletter and contact forms.
  • Automatic information — standard log data and cookie identifiers collected by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages), Google AdSense and embedded services such as YouTube, including IP address, browser type, pages viewed and referring URLs.

Our calculators run entirely in your browser; the numbers you type into them are not sent to us.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request and, when you consent, to share a quote or home-value request with up to three matched local providers.
  • To administer contests, process pledges, review applications and publish approved events and listings.
  • To send newsletters you request (unsubscribe anytime).
  • To operate, secure and improve the site, and to show advertising.

We do not sell personal information for money. Sharing a lead with a provider happens only at your request and with your consent.

Cookies and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Our cookie banner lets you choose "Essential only," which requests non-personalised ads. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a Google-certified consent message where required.

YouTube videos load only after you press play and use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.

Service providers

Forms are delivered by a third-party form-relay service that forwards submissions by email; hosting is provided by GitHub Pages; fonts by Google Fonts; ads by Google AdSense. Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

Retention and security

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose collected, for consent records, or as required by law. All pages are served over HTTPS. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent to be contacted, using the contact form. Reply STOP to any text message to opt out. Residents of states with consumer privacy laws (such as California) may have additional rights; we honour verified requests.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and contests and applications are limited to people 18 and older.

Changes

We'll post any changes here with a new effective date.
