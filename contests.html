Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase necessary. A purchase or donation does not improve your chances of winning.
  2. Eligibility: legal U.S. residents 18+. Employees of 66221.com and sponsors, and their households, are not eligible.
  3. Entry period: each contest's window is announced on this page; late entries are not considered.
  4. Limit: one entry per person per contest period.
  5. Judging: a panel scores entries on originality, quality and local relevance (photo/yard) or community impact (hero). Public voting, when used, counts for no more than 30%.
  6. Prizes: as listed; no cash alternative; non-transferable except where required by law. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  7. Notification: winners are notified by email and must respond within 14 days or an alternate is chosen.
  8. Licence: entrants keep copyright but grant 66221.com a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display the entry with credit on the site and its social channels.
  9. Privacy: entry data is handled under our Privacy Policy.
  10. Void where prohibited. We may cancel or modify a contest if fraud or technical issues compromise fairness.
