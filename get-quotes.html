Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Homeowners never pay us. Providers may pay a referral or advertising fee, which is how we keep the site free. See How we make money.

How many people will contact me?

No more than three providers per request, and only for the service you chose.

Can I request quotes for a rental or a home I'm buying?

Yes — choose "Buying soon" or note the situation in your description.

Are you a contractor?

No. 66221.com is an independent information site. We don't perform work, and each provider is responsible for its own services, licensing and warranties.
