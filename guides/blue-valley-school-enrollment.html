Enrolling in Blue Valley schools from 66221

By the 66221 Living Editorial Desk · Updated October 21, 2026 · 7-minute read

Moving into 66221 with school-age children? Enrollment in Blue Valley USD 229 is straightforward once you know the order of operations: confirm the boundary, gather proof of residency, and get health records in early.

Step 1 — Confirm your attendance area

School assignment follows your street address, not your ZIP code. Use the district's online school locator and save a screenshot of the result. Fast-growing districts adjust boundaries as new schools open, so confirm again if you're buying a home in a newer subdivision. When a house sits near a boundary street, call the district's enrollment office.

Step 2 — Gather your documents

Districts in Kansas typically ask for:

  • Proof of residency — a closing statement, signed lease or recent utility bill in the parent's name at the new address. If you're under contract but haven't closed, ask the district what interim documentation it accepts.
  • Child's birth certificate or other proof of age.
  • Parent/guardian photo ID.
  • Immunization records meeting Kansas requirements, and a health assessment for new Kansas students where required.
  • Prior school records, including any IEP or 504 plan, so services continue without a gap.

Step 3 — Register online

Blue Valley uses an online enrollment system for new families. Create a parent account, complete the forms for each child and upload documents. The school office then confirms placement and next steps.

Kindergarten timing

In Kansas, a child must turn five on or before August 31 to start kindergarten that school year. Many districts hold kindergarten roundup events in late winter or spring — watch district announcements from January onward.

Moving mid-year

Mid-year transfers happen all the time. Request records from the current school before you leave, keep a copy of the most recent report card, and contact the new school a week or two ahead so counselors can plan schedules — especially important for middle schoolers with advanced math or language sequences.

Special programs

Blue Valley offers gifted services, special education, English language learner programs and career-focused academies at the high school level through its Center for Advanced Professional Studies. Ask about eligibility and application windows early; some programs fill or test on fixed calendars.

Before-school checklist

  • ☐ School locator result saved
  • ☐ Residency proof ready (closing statement or lease)
  • ☐ Birth certificate and parent ID
  • ☐ Immunizations up to date
  • ☐ Records/IEP requested from prior school
  • ☐ Online enrollment submitted
  • ☐ Transportation and before/after-care arranged
