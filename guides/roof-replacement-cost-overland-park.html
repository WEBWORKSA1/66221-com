Roof replacement cost in Overland Park

By the 66221 Living Editorial Desk · Updated October 21, 2026 · 8-minute read

For a typical 66221 two-story with an attached three-car garage, a full asphalt-shingle replacement generally lands somewhere between the mid-teens and the low thirty-thousands. Here's what moves a bid up or down — and how to tell a fair quote from a padded one.

The unit roofers price in: the "square"

Roofers measure in squares: one square covers 100 square feet of roof surface. Roof area is larger than your home's footprint because of pitch and overhangs. A 2,200 sq ft footprint with a moderate 6/12–8/12 pitch commonly works out to roughly 28–32 squares; complex rooflines with many valleys and dormers add more, plus waste.

What pushes the price up

  • Pitch and height. Steep roofs need harnesses, roof jacks and more labour time.
  • Complexity. Valleys, hips, dormers, turrets and skylights each add flashing and waste.
  • Decking. Rotted or delaminated OSB is replaced per sheet once the old roof comes off. Good contracts state a per-sheet price in advance.
  • Code items. Ice-and-water shield, drip edge and ventilation upgrades required by current code.
  • Gutters, vents and accessories. Often damaged in the same hailstorm.
  • Post-storm demand. After a major hail event, schedules stretch and prices rise.

Permits and HOA approval

Overland Park requires a permit for roof replacement, and reputable contractors pull it themselves. Most 66221 subdivisions also have HOA architectural guidelines that restrict colours and sometimes materials — get written approval before ordering shingles.

How to compare three bids

  1. Same scope. Confirm each bid includes the same number of squares, underlayment type, ventilation, flashing, drip edge and gutter work.
  2. Named products. Brand and product line for shingles and underlayment, plus manufacturer warranty tier.
  3. Workmanship warranty. Length and what voids it.
  4. Decking pricing. A stated price per sheet prevents surprise bills.
  5. Payment schedule. A modest deposit is normal; large up-front cash demands are a red flag.
  6. Local presence. Office address, years in business and references from nearby streets.

Repair or replace?

Spot repairs make sense for isolated wind damage or a few missing shingles on a roof with plenty of life left. Widespread hail bruising, granule loss across slopes, or a roof past roughly 18–20 years usually points to replacement — especially if insurance is paying for it.
