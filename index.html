Common questions about 66221

Where exactly is ZIP 66221?

It covers part of south Overland Park in Johnson County, Kansas, roughly between the 135th Street corridor and 175th Street, plus a small portion of Aubry Township. Boundaries are set by the Postal Service and don't match city, school or tax-unit lines exactly — check a specific address before relying on it.

Which school district serves 66221?

Most addresses fall within Blue Valley Unified School District 229. Elementary and middle schools located inside the ZIP include Sunset Ridge, Morse and Harmony elementaries and Aubry Bend and Harmony middle schools. Attendance areas are set by the district, so always confirm with its school locator. More on schools.

How much are property taxes on a 66221 home?

Kansas taxes homes at 11.5% of appraised value multiplied by the combined mill levy of every taxing body for your address. As a rough guide, budget around 1.4% of market value a year. Use our property tax estimator and your actual statement for precision.
